Case study 01 · Marketplace / Business strategy

FarmSetu

Connecting Telangana farmers to Hyderabad communities.

Live product ↗
Illustrative agriculture image · not field research photography

The opportunity

More value for farmers. Competitive prices for households.

Farmers reported prices around ₹28/kg, while observed Hyderabad retail prices ranged from ₹60–85/kg. I explored whether aggregating community demand could reduce the gap.

10–15Farmer conversations in 4 villages
79–96%Target selling-price uplift, not realized impact
7Simulated order scenarios
01 / Discovery

Listen before designing

Spoke with farmers in Devunipally, Dhulikatta, Eligedu and Choppadandi. Identified pricing disparity and limited direct market access as opportunities for further validation.

02 / Strategy

Design viable unit economics

Proposed ₹50–55/kg for farmers and ₹60–70/kg delivered pricing, allowing ₹10–15/kg for handling and logistics. Prioritized group sourcing to aggregate demand.

03 / Execution

Build the two-sided MVP

Defined buyer and farmer journeys, listings, quotation comparison, inventory views and six-stage order tracking. Built a database-backed marketplace with React and Supabase.

04 / Validation

Test before commercial rollout

Database checks covered six test farmer profiles, six additional sourcing plans and seven simulated order scenarios. Next: verify costs, activate suppliers and run a real group purchase.

PRODUCT DECISION

Group purchasing over individual delivery

Aggregating orders can spread transport and handling costs across households. Prioritized sourcing groups, quotations and order visibility; deferred payments and logistics integration until costs and demand are validated.

Measure next: Farmer price realization, buyer delivered price, group-to-order conversion and on-time fulfilment.

ReactSupabase / PostgreSQLVercelPricing strategyProcurement analytics

Status: Deployed MVP. Pricing targets depend on comparable rice grades, confirmed farm-price units and actual fulfilment costs; commercial impact is not yet measured.

Case study 02 · Education / AI-assisted learning

PathPilot

A career readiness platform for engineering students.

The opportunity

From learning a skill to being ready for a role.

Students described fragmented technical preparation. I designed a unified experience linking career direction, practice, practical evidence and progress visibility.

25Student interviews across 5 colleges
10 weeksRole-specific learning roadmaps
5Technical career domains
01 / Discovery

Identify the readiness gap

Student conversations included BVRIT, VNR VJIET and GRIET. Early feedback from five placement aspirants informed the product direction.

02 / Strategy

Prioritize structured practice

Translated research into requirements, user journeys and prioritization. Focused the MVP on career roadmaps, assessments, practical tasks and progress tracking.

03 / Execution

Make learning actionable

Built guided learning plans, readiness estimates, assignment workflows, simulated work and optional Firebase student accounts. Adaptive AI requires an optional local backend.

04 / Validation

Define a measurable pilot

Planned a four-week pilot for five students: 80% activation, 75% week-two retention, 60% assignment completion and a 15-point assessment improvement target.

PRODUCT DECISION

Practice-first over a content-only platform

Prioritized role-based roadmaps, practical assignments and readiness feedback over a broad content catalogue. Optional AI and account features support the core learning workflow.

Measure next: Activation, week-two retention, assignment completion and matched pre/post assessment scores.

React / ViteFirebaseVercelGuided AI workflowsLearning analytics

Status: Public prototype. Pilot metrics are proposed targets; learning improvement and retention have not yet been measured.

Case study 03 · Healthcare / Workflow design

CareCompanion

Connected doctor–patient care management.

The opportunity

A prescription should not be the end of the care journey.

I explored a two-sided product that links the consultation to clear instructions, ongoing care tasks and a way for patients to flag confusion.

10Functional areas in the latest prototype
7Prescription Studio tabs
2Doctor and patient experiences
01 / Problem framing

Connect fragmented steps

Mapped clinician and patient jobs-to-be-done across registration, consultation, prescription, after-visit guidance and clarification. The need is a product hypothesis.

02 / Product design

Structure the care experience

Designed the seven-tab Prescription Studio with medication schedules, multilingual guidance, dietary instructions, precautions and downloadable handouts.

03 / Execution

Link records and care tasks

Developed doctor and patient modules, chronological care records, descriptive analytics and a prescription import workflow with patient matching and duplicate prevention.

04 / Responsible delivery

Measure clarity, not clinical claims

Designed clarification feedback and reminder check-ins. Next: test task completion, instruction comprehension and workflow reliability using fictional records.

PRODUCT DECISION

Continuity of care over expanding clinical scope

Prioritized connecting prescriptions, care tasks and patient clarification instead of automated diagnosis or a full clinical record system. Kept the demonstration browser-local with fictional data.

Measure next: Completion of core tasks, instruction clarity and reliable patient-to-prescription matching.

React / ViteJavaScriptBrowser-local storageHealthcare analyticsVercel

Status: Functional demo using fictional data, not a clinical system. The public demo is an earlier build; the latest source changes are not yet verified as deployed.
